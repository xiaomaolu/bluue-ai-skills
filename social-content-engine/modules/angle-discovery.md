# Angle Discovery

Research answers “what happened?” Angle discovery answers “why is this worth saying?”

## Core chain

Use:

`Fact → Pattern → Tension → Implication → Angle`

Example:

- Fact: Apple enters foldables later than major competitors.
- Pattern: competitors spent years developing the category.
- Tension: late entry normally looks like a disadvantage.
- Implication: Apple can enter after much early technical and consumer experimentation.
- Angle: Apple’s biggest advantage in foldables may be arriving late.

## Candidate families

Internally consider up to five:

1. **Observation** — a concrete detail people may overlook
2. **Implication** — what this development changes
3. **Contrarian** — a defensible interpretation that differs from the obvious take
4. **Comparison** — competitor, history, product cycle, or adjacent category
5. **User impact** — what changes for actual users

Do not expose all five by default.

## Scoring

Score candidates internally:

- novelty: 25
- specificity: 25
- evidence: 20
- audience fit: 15
- platform fit: 15

Prefer the highest combined score, not the most dramatic wording.

## Replaceability test

Ask:

> Could this sentence be reused for another company or product by changing the name?

Low-specificity example:

> Apple’s entry will increase competition.

High-specificity example:

> Apple can enter foldables after competitors have spent years teaching consumers what the category is for.

## Generic-angle penalty

Strongly penalize:

- competition will increase
- users will have more choices
- innovation keeps accelerating
- the future is exciting
- this may reshape the industry
- the market is evolving

unless followed by a concrete mechanism or consequence.

## Evidence alignment

The angle may be stronger than any single fact, but it must remain defensible.

Do not convert:

- rumor → certainty
- correlation → causation
- community chatter → market-wide consensus
- one anecdote → general behavior

## Multilingual angle generation

Discover the underlying idea before phrasing it.

When writing in another language, regenerate the angle naturally in the target language. Do not preserve English rhetorical structures merely because the research was in English.

## Output

Internally return:

- recommended angle
- up to 2 backup angles if genuinely distinct
- strongest supporting facts
- angle confidence
- claims that need qualification

The writer receives the recommended angle unless the user asks to choose.
