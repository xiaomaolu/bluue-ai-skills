# Repurpose

Repurposing preserves the idea while rebuilding the delivery for a new platform, format, audience, or language.

## Preserve

- core thesis
- validated evidence
- claim confidence
- user voice
- required facts
- factual caveats

## Rebuild

- opening
- order
- length
- paragraph shape
- level of explanation
- CTA
- hashtags
- spoken/written rhythm
- platform-native conventions

Do not simply delete paragraphs from LinkedIn to make an X post.

## Cross-language repurpose

When platform and language both change:

1. recover the underlying thesis and evidence
2. apply the destination platform profile
3. apply destination-language conventions
4. transcreate
5. review for `TRANSLATIONESE`

Example:

English LinkedIn → Vietnamese Facebook should become a locally natural Facebook post, not a literal Vietnamese translation of LinkedIn formatting.

## Research reuse

Reuse previous research when:

- the topic is unchanged
- the evidence is still current enough
- the new platform does not require new claims

Run research again only when the destination request introduces new facts, new recency, or a new market context.

## Output

Return one platform-native version unless the user requests multiple variants.
