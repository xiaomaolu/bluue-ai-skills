# Writer

Write from the prepared brief. Do not restart research or strategy unless the state is internally inconsistent.

## Modes

### direct
Use when topic, angle, platform, and evidence are clear.

Generate one draft.

### exploratory
Use when wording is uncertain.

Generate up to three internally distinct drafts, score them, and keep only the best. Do not expose three near-identical options by default.

### precision
Use for:

- finance
- regulation
- compliance
- legal
- health
- political or public-policy content
- brand-critical factual claims
- high-stakes product claims

Use conservative wording and explicit attribution where needed.

## Writing rules

- Lead with information, tension, or a meaningful observation.
- Keep one primary thesis in a short post.
- Use concrete nouns and plain verbs.
- Vary sentence length naturally.
- Remove introductions that only announce the topic.
- Prefer a specific implication to a generic summary.
- Do not force all research into the post.
- Do not force a question at the end.
- Do not add hashtags by default.
- Do not manufacture urgency.

## Evidence budgets

These are defaults, not hard limits:

- X: 1–2 major claims, 0–2 useful numbers
- LinkedIn: up to 3–4 major claims
- Instagram caption: only evidence that supports the visual/content premise
- Threads: 1–2 major claims
- Reddit: more context/evidence is acceptable when discussion benefits
- short-video script: enough evidence to support the spoken payoff without turning into a list
- Xiaohongshu: practical details and concrete takeaways are often more useful than formal citation density

## Human voice

Prefer:

- plain language
- natural transitions
- restrained confidence
- specific observations
- a clear perspective

Flag or rewrite default filler such as:

- It is worth noting that
- In today’s rapidly evolving landscape
- This marks a significant milestone
- Only time will tell
- Exciting times ahead

Use `references/anti-slop.md` for multilingual patterns.

## Multilingual writing

Write natively in the target language.

Do not draft in English and translate sentence by sentence unless the user asked for literal translation.

Preserve:

- brand/product names
- ticker symbols
- technical terms that are conventionally left untranslated
- user-provided terminology
- local punctuation and spacing conventions
- requested code-switching

For cross-language content, transcreate the underlying thesis and evidence.

## First-person content

Use first person only when supported by the user’s request or supplied source.

Allowed:

> 我发现 30 秒合约里……

when the user explicitly says these are their own observations.

Do not add:

> 我做了三年 / 我测试了上千笔

unless supplied.

## Output

Return only the publishable draft to review. Internal alternatives remain hidden.
