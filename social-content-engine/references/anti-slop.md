# Anti-Slop Patterns

These patterns are warning signs, not absolute banned-word lists. Replace them when they add little information.

## English

Watch for:

- redefine the industry
- game changer
- revolutionary
- usher in a new era
- rapidly evolving landscape
- highly anticipated
- continues to gain momentum
- exciting development
- set a new benchmark
- the future looks promising
- only time will tell
- it is worth noting that
- in today's world

Repair:
replace abstraction with a fact, mechanism, comparison, or implication.

## Simplified Chinese

Watch for:

- 重新定义行业
- 颠覆行业
- 开启新时代
- 持续攀升
- 备受期待
- 树立全新标杆
- 引领未来
- 未来可期
- 值得注意的是
- 不难发现
- 在当今快速发展的……
- 从某种意义上说
- 这不仅是……更是……

Repair:
写清楚“具体变了什么、为什么、影响谁”。

## Traditional Chinese

Also watch for literal conversion of Mainland marketing phrases. Localize vocabulary, not characters alone.

## Vietnamese

Watch for translated corporate filler such as:

- đánh dấu một cột mốc quan trọng
- mở ra một kỷ nguyên mới
- thay đổi cuộc chơi
- trong bối cảnh không ngừng phát triển
- tương lai đầy hứa hẹn

Use concrete Vietnamese explanation instead.

## Japanese

Watch for over-translated phrases such as:

- 新たな時代を切り開く
- 業界を再定義する
- ゲームチェンジャー
- 今後の展開に期待が高まる

Use restrained, specific claims unless promotional tone is requested.

## Korean

Watch for:

- 새로운 시대를 열다
- 업계를 재정의하다
- 게임 체인저
- 빠르게 변화하는 환경 속에서
- 기대감이 커지고 있다

Prefer a concrete mechanism or user consequence.

## Structural slop

Also flag:

- three paragraphs that restate the same thesis
- a conclusion that only repeats the opening
- a generic question added solely for engagement
- a list of facts with no angle
- excessive symmetrical sentence patterns
- “first, second, third” when the content is too short to need formal structure
- invented authority (“as an expert…”) without user support
