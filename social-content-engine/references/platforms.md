# Platform Profiles

These are defaults. User instructions and actual community norms override them.

Platform limits and algorithms change. Do not invent current hard limits when the task does not require them.

## X / Twitter

**Default:** concise, high information density, one main idea.

Structure:
- observation / claim
- minimum context
- implication
- optional close

Prefer:
- opinion early
- specific information
- sharp comparison
- compact paragraphs

Avoid:
- long setup
- generic questions
- corporate language
- hashtag stuffing
- repeated conclusion

CTA: optional.

## LinkedIn

**Default:** professional, explanatory, medium-high density.

Structure:
- observation
- context
- analysis
- implication

Prefer:
- product/business/industry insight
- evidence that supports the argument
- readable paragraph blocks

Avoid:
- fake leadership wisdom
- motivational clichés
- “I’m humbled/excited to announce” unless authentic
- excessive one-line paragraphs for drama

CTA: optional and specific.

## Instagram

**Default:** visual-first. Caption supports the asset.

Prefer:
- strong first line
- emotional or practical relevance
- scannable blocks
- save/share value when genuine

Avoid:
- treating the caption like a LinkedIn essay
- irrelevant hashtags
- repeating every detail visible in the image

For Reels, use the short-video profile.

## Threads

**Default:** conversational, compact, opinion-oriented.

Prefer:
- natural voice
- quick context
- a view worth responding to

Avoid:
- formal essay structure
- forced engagement bait

## Reddit

**Default:** participant voice, context-rich enough to be credible, low marketing polish.

Prefer:
- concrete details
- uncertainty when real
- evidence where relevant
- genuine discussion
- subreddit-aware formatting when known

Avoid:
- brand copy
- fake expertise
- hidden promotion
- generic CTA
- excessive headings for a short post

First-person claims must come from the user.

## Facebook

**Default:** accessible, contextual, community-friendly.

Prefer:
- understandable setup
- personal/community relevance
- slightly more explanation than X

For Vietnamese or Southeast Asian consumer audiences, local conversational tone may matter more than a global corporate voice.

## TikTok / Douyin / Instagram Reels

**Default:** spoken script, immediate payoff.

Structure:
- hook
- context within seconds
- development
- payoff
- optional CTA

Prefer:
- spoken rhythm
- short clauses
- visual beats or scene cues when requested

Avoid:
- essay syntax
- long throat-clearing intro
- reading a list of facts with no narrative

Douyin localization may differ from TikTok; follow the requested market.

## YouTube Shorts

Use short-video logic, with a slightly clearer explanatory payoff when the topic benefits from it.

## YouTube long-form

Focus on:
- title premise
- first-minute retention
- narrative sequence
- section transitions
- payoff
- evidence placement

Do not use short-post compression for long-form scripts.

## Xiaohongshu / RED

**Default:** practical, personal-feeling, visual/content-card friendly.

Prefer:
- concrete benefit or curiosity in title/opening
- structured takeaways
- useful detail
- natural Chinese
- authentic experience only when supplied

Avoid:
- fake personal experience
- corporate press-release tone
- hollow “宝藏/绝绝子” slang unless it fits the user’s voice
- keyword stuffing

For product/experience posts, clarify what is observation vs first-hand experience.

## Weibo

**Default:** timely, compact, public-discussion friendly.

Prefer:
- quick context
- clear viewpoint
- topic relevance
- natural Chinese internet rhythm when appropriate

Avoid:
- long formal preamble
- excessive marketing hashtags

## Telegram / Discord

**Default:** community update / discussion message.

Prefer:
- direct information
- scannable bullets when useful
- clear links or actions when supplied

Avoid:
- public-social-media engagement bait
- over-polished brand language unless it is an announcement channel

## Generic / unknown platform

Use:
- one clear thesis
- concise context
- readable paragraphs
- medium information density
- optional CTA
- no default hashtags

Do not assert unknown algorithmic or formatting rules.
